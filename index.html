In an emergency, do not wait for an appointment

Call your local emergency number straight away (for example 112 in India, 911 in the US) or go to the nearest hospital. This website cannot handle emergencies.

Seek emergency care for:
  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Sudden weakness, confusion or trouble speaking
  • Severe bleeding or loss of consciousness
